You never cast a spell. You cast a rainbow.

Dawn strikes your horn and leaves it as seven bands of light. Each band is a verb. They fire together, always, in a rigid fan, so every shot is seven decisions at once and you aim the whole spectrum or none of it.

**Controls**

- WASD or arrows to walk the valley
- Q and E to narrow or widen the fan
- SPACE to flare: the bands reach further and hit harder, paid out of vigour
- ENTER to walk into the Prism and back out
- 1 to 6 at the Prism to spend Light
- SHIFT+R to begin again from wave 1

**What is in the 13KB**

The band order is spectral and never changes, so unsealing a new one reshapes every shot you have already learned to aim. Between waves the Prism sells you the next colour. There is a save system, so a run survives the tab closing.

There is not one asset file in the zip. Every frame of art is drawn procedurally at runtime and every sound is synthesised, because at 13,312 bytes a single PNG would be thirty times the entire budget. The build is 11,274 bytes, so about 2KB of the cap is unused.

Built with AI assistance. The source in the repo is unmangled and the build script is there, so you can rebuild the exact archive yourself.
